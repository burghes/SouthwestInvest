The South West is a large, connected region with land for new development, a growing clean energy base and research strengths in defence, life sciences and space. Investors get one front door and partners who work as one region.

Room to grow

Employment land, science parks, enterprise zones and freeport tax sites, from city edges to coastal ports. Sites for labs, factories, data and logistics.

Clean power

New nuclear at Hinkley Point C, floating offshore wind in the Celtic Sea, and geothermal energy and lithium in Cornwall. Low-carbon power and the supply chains that come with it.

Skills and research

Universities in Exeter, Plymouth, Bournemouth and Falmouth. National labs at Porton Down. The Met Office in Exeter. Technical Excellence Colleges for defence in Plymouth and Yeovil.

Quality of life

Two national parks, the Jurassic Coast and an Atlantic and Channel coastline. Places people want to live, which helps you hire and keep talent.

Connected to the UK and the world

M4, M5, A303 and A30 road links. Mainline rail to London. Airports at Exeter, Newquay and Bournemouth. Ports at Plymouth, Poole, Portland and Falmouth.

Investment already under way

Major public and private projects are already changing the region's economy. They create supply chain, skills and site opportunities for the businesses that follow.

  • Bridgwater, SomersetAgratas battery gigafactory

    £380m of government investment in one of Europe's largest planned battery cell plants, expected to create more than 4,200 direct jobs.1

  • Celtic SeaFloating offshore wind

    Three 1.5GW floating wind sites now under lease off South Wales and the South West: up to 4.5GW in the first phase.2

  • Plymouth, DevonDefence Growth Deal

    £50m to expand Plymouth's strengths in maritime autonomy and defence skills, alongside HM Naval Base Devonport.3

  • Somerset coastHinkley Point C

    New nuclear power station with Unit 1 planned to operate from 2030. More than 1,700 apprentices trained, most from the South West.4

  • CornwallCritical minerals

    A £43m geothermal lithium facility backed by the government's DRIVE35 fund, part of a growing minerals cluster.5

  • Goonhilly, CornwallDeep space communications

    Goonhilly Earth Station provides deep space communications for the UK Space Agency and international partners.6

  • Porton Down, WiltshirePorton Science Park

    Labs and grow-on space next to Dstl and the UK Health Security Agency, with Life Sciences Opportunity Zone status.7

  • Winfrith, DorsetDorset Innovation Park

    Enterprise zone with a dedicated delivery company, aiming for 300 new jobs on site by 2029.8

Clean energy

From new nuclear to floating wind, the South West is building a big part of the UK's future energy system.

  • Hinkley Point C in Somerset, with Unit 1 planned to operate from 2030.
  • The first commercial floating offshore wind sites in the Celtic Sea, up to 4.5GW.
  • Geothermal energy and lithium extraction in Cornwall's granite.
  • Supply chain openings in fabrication, marine services, grid and storage.
Where
Somerset coast, Cornwall, Dorset, ports around the peninsula

Defence, security and cyber

A long-standing defence base that is moving fast into autonomy, cyber and dual-use technology.

  • HM Naval Base Devonport and Plymouth's Defence Growth Deal for maritime autonomy.
  • Dstl at Porton Down: defence science, chemical and biological security.
  • Helicopter design and manufacture in Yeovil, and Defence Technical Excellence Colleges in Plymouth and Yeovil.
  • Training estates and test ranges on land and at sea.
Where
Plymouth, Porton Down, Yeovil, Salisbury Plain, Dorset coast

High-value manufacturing, from battery cells to aerospace and marine systems.

  • Aerospace and rotorcraft engineering in Somerset.
  • Marine manufacturing and boatbuilding around Poole, Plymouth and Falmouth.
  • Supplier opportunities in automation, materials and precision engineering.

Space, data and communications

A peninsula with clear access to the Atlantic, the skies and global data links.

  • Goonhilly Earth Station: commercial deep space communications.
  • Spaceport Cornwall at Cornwall Airport Newquay.
  • The Met Office in Exeter: weather and climate science and supercomputing.
  • Subsea cable landings and data infrastructure on the Cornish coast.

Built around national priorities

We work alongside the Department for Business and Trade and its Office for Investment so that investors get joined-up support from national and local government.

Industrial Strategy

Government is targeting investment at eight growth-driving sectors (the IS-8). Clean energy, defence, advanced manufacturing, life sciences, digital and creative are all strengths here.1

Lower power costs

From 2027 the British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme aims to cut electricity costs for around 10,000 eligible manufacturers by about a quarter.1

Faster sites

The Strategic Sites Accelerator and planning reform aim to bring investment-ready sites to market sooner. We will put South West sites forward.1
